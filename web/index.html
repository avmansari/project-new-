FAQ

How do I claim?

Paste your Robinhood Chain wallet address, press Claim, connect a wallet and confirm. Tokens go straight to the address you entered.

Is there a fee?

Each claim includes a small claim fee (about $0.10) plus normal network gas. Your wallet shows the exact amount before you confirm anything.

When can I trade?

Claimed CHOMP stays in your wallet until trading opens at launch. The date will be announced on X first.

Which network?

Robinhood Chain. When you connect, your wallet is asked to add or switch to it.

Is this safe?

We will never ask for your seed phrase or private key. The claim only needs one wallet confirmation. Always check you are on the official site and the contract address shown below.

Can I claim for a friend's wallet?

Yes. Anyone can submit the claim, but CHOMP always goes to the wallet that is eligible, never to the sender.
