FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is this?

A Bitcoin-style Proof-of-Work token on Robinhood Chain. Instead of big mining farms, anyone can mine from a browser. Each mined block also writes an inscription record on-chain.

Can I mine on my phone?

Yes. Open the site in your wallet app's browser (MetaMask, Trust, Coinbase…) or connect with WalletConnect. Keep the screen on while mining; phones are slower than PCs, so expect fewer blocks.

Do I need a GPU?

No. The CPU miner works everywhere. If your browser supports WebGPU (Chrome/Edge on desktop and Android), your graphics card can mine too and is usually much faster. The app shows which GPU it detected and has a Benchmark button to measure your speed.

I found a block but didn't get it. Why?

Mining is a race: the first valid claim on the blockchain wins. If another miner's claim lands first, yours is rejected and mining moves on to the next block. Turn on Auto-claim to claim as fast as possible.

What is a "lot"?

1 lot = 5,000 tokens = one mined block. The marketplace trades whole lots only, priced in US dollars and paid in ETH.

What does it cost?

Mining is free. Transactions need a little ETH for network gas, and marketplace trades have a 2% fee. Your wallet always shows the exact amount before you confirm.

Which wallets work?

MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX, Trust, Phantom, Brave and other browser wallets are detected automatically; mobile wallets can connect via WalletConnect.

Is it safe?

The smart contracts are open source and have been through an internal security review with automated tests. They are experimental software: only use funds you can afford to lose, and read the Terms & risk disclaimer. Nothing here is financial advice.

What happens after all 21M tokens are mined?

Mining stops. Tokens can still be transferred and traded on the marketplace.

Is this affiliated with Robinhood?

No. This is an independent project built on Robinhood Chain, a public blockchain. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Robinhood Markets, Inc.
