FAQ

How do I claim?

Paste your Robinhood Chain wallet address, press Claim, and confirm in your connected wallet. Each claim costs a small fee of about $0.10 (shown on the page) plus network gas. Tokens go straight to the address you entered.

When can I trade?

Trading opens at launch after the date is announced on X. Claimed tokens stay in your wallet until then.

Which network?

Robinhood Chain. Your wallet will be asked to switch to it.
